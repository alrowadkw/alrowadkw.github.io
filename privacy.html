Privacy Policy - Al Rowad Warehouse Bot

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Who uses it

The Al Rowad Warehouse Bot is a private tool used only by the owner of Al Rowad, Kuwait. It is not offered to the public and no other person can sign in to it.

Google data we use

The bot asks Google for one permission only: to send email from the owner's own Gmail account (the gmail.send scope). It sends loading emails that the owner has seen and approved. It does not read, search, change or delete any email, and it does not access contacts, files or any other Google data.

What is stored

The bot keeps a private record of the emails it sent (subject, date and status) on the owner's own computer, for the owner's business records. The Google sign-in token is kept only in the owner's private settings file. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing access

The owner can remove the bot's access at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access.
